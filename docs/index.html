What it does

Task switch

A Haiku judge spots a new task. ghosted_ archives the old chat, runs /clear, restores your prompt and starts it.

Long runs

Optional: past a limit you choose, it compacts mid-run and types Continue where you left off.

Terse answers

On by default: compressed notes in the terminal, about 20% less text and cost in tests. Switch with /ghosted full, lite or off.

Recall

Every cleared conversation is archived. /ghosted:recall <words> brings back a short digest.

How it works

your prompt ──► hook
                 ├─ small context · slash command · "same:" · obvious follow-up ──► passes
                 └─ Haiku judge: new task?
                      ├─ no  ──► passes
                      └─ yes ──► archive · save prompt · /clear
                                   └─► restore prompt + carried facts ──► "Start the pending task."

The judge costs about $0.0013 per switch and loads none of your settings, plugins or MCP servers. Errors fail open: your prompt goes through as if ghosted_ weren't installed.
